The problem

Crop selection depends on multiple field conditions. SmartCrop AI brings those measurements into one simple decision-support flow.

01

Many variables

Nutrients, rainfall, temperature, humidity and pH all appear together in crop-selection data.

02

Hard to compare

Raw measurements are difficult to translate quickly into several possible crop choices.

03

Need clear output

Farmers and demonstrators need a result they can understand, compare and explore.

How it works

One complete loop from field data to an explainable recommendation.

01 · INPUT

Field data

Enter N, P, K, temperature, humidity, pH and rainfall.

02 · ANALYZE

ML model

Random Forest evaluates the seven parameters together.

03 · RECOMMEND

Crop ranking

Return the leading crop classes with model scores.

04 · EXPLORE

Understand & test

Compare crops, inspect the reasoning and run what-if scenarios.
